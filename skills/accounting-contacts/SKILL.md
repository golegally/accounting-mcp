# Contacts

- `contacts_list`: List contacts with pagination
- `contacts_create`: Create a new contact (customer or vendor)
- `contacts_bulk_delete_contacts`: Delete multiple contacts
- `contacts_get_by_reference`: Get a contact by the id it has in the system it came from
- `contacts_validate_bulk_delete_contacts`: Validate which contacts can be deleted
- `contacts_get`: Get a contact
- `contacts_edit`: Edit a contact
- `contacts_delete`: Delete a contact
- `contacts_merge`: Merge a customer into another
- `contacts_edit_opening_balance`: Edit the opening balance of a contact
