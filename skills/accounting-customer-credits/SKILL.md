# Customer Credits

- `customer_credits_get_balances`: Get customer credit balances
- `customer_credits_apply`: Apply customer credit balance to an invoice
- `customer_credits_unapply`: Take a credit application back off its invoice
- `customer_credits_refund`: Refund customer credit balance to bank
- `customer_credits_topup`: Top up customer credit balance
- `customer_credits_list_transactions`: List customer credit transaction history (paginated)
