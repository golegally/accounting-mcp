# Document Reporting

- `document_reporting_terms`: Whether a registration reports its invoices to the tax authority
- `document_reporting_report`: Report an issued invoice to the tax authority
- `document_reporting_cancel`: Cancel an invoice's report at the tax authority, voiding the invoice
