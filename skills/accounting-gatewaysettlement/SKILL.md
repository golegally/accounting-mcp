# GatewaySettlement

- `gateway_settlement_list_settlements`: List recorded settlements
- `gateway_settlement_record_settlement`: Record a settlement a payment account has paid out
- `gateway_settlement_get_pending_amounts`: Captured payments not yet settled, by payment account
