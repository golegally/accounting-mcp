# Movement Documents

- `bill_movement_of_bill`: The government's document a supplier generated for a bill's goods
- `bill_movement_read`: Find at the government the document for a bill's goods
- `bill_movement_reject`: Reject the document a supplier generated for a bill's goods
- `consolidated_movements_list`: A registration's consolidated movement documents
- `consolidated_movements_consolidate`: Consolidate invoices' movement documents going in one conveyance
- `consolidated_movements_get`: A consolidated movement document
- `consolidated_movements_refresh`: Read a consolidated movement document again from the government
- `consolidated_movements_regenerate`: Make a consolidated movement document again for another conveyance
- `movement_documents_stops`: Registrations whose movement documents stop for missed returns
- `movement_documents_terms`: Whether a registration's goods move on the government's document
- `movement_documents_transporter`: Check a transporter's id at the government
- `movement_documents_transporter_contact`: Check a transporter's email or mobile at the register
- `movement_documents_transporter_enrolment`: The enrolment a transporter's id names
- `movement_documents_issue`: Generate the government's document for an invoice's goods
- `movement_documents_cancel`: Cancel an invoice's movement document
- `consolidated_movements_of_invoice`: The consolidated movement documents an invoice's goods go under
- `movement_documents_extend`: Extend the validity of an invoice's movement document
- `movement_documents_printed`: Where an invoice's movement document is read as printed
- `movement_documents_invoice_terms`: Whether an invoice's goods need the government's document
- `movement_documents_change_transporter`: Give an invoice's movement document to another transporter
- `movement_documents_change_vehicle`: Put an invoice's goods on another vehicle
- `vehicle_groups_list`: An invoice's goods moving in several vehicles
- `vehicle_groups_start`: Split an invoice's goods across vehicles
- `vehicle_groups_add_vehicle`: Add a vehicle to a group
- `vehicle_groups_change_vehicle`: Replace a vehicle of a group
