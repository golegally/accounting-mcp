# Payment reminders

- `customer_notifications_get`: A customer's notifications
- `payment_reminders_set_customer`: Exclude a customer from payment reminders and invoice messages, or include them again
- `payment_reminders_set_invoice`: Exclude an invoice from payment reminders, or include it again
- `payment_reminders_settings`: Whether customers get payment reminders and invoice messages, and on which days
- `payment_reminders_save`: Switch payment reminders on or off, and set their schedule
