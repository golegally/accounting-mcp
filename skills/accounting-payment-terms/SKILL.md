# Payment terms

- `commitments_list_commitments`: A customer's commitments, latest first
- `commitments_create_commitment`: Record a commitment and invoice the part of it already due
- `payment_terms_standing`: A customer's payment terms, and what they have paid in, owe, used and may still spend
- `payment_terms_set_terms`: Set how a customer pays each invoice
