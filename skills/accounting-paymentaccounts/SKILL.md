# PaymentAccounts

- `payment_accounts_list`: The organization's payment accounts, without their keys
- `payment_accounts_create`: Add a payment account
- `payment_accounts_list_brands`: The accounts each brand takes payments with
- `payment_accounts_set_brand`: Choose the accounts a brand takes payments with
- `payment_accounts_update`: Rename a payment account, switch its mode, or change where its payments are deposited
- `payment_accounts_remove`: Remove a payment account no brand uses and that has taken no payment
