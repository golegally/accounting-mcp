# PaymentGatewayInvoice

- `payment_gateway_invoice_initiate_payment`: Open a payment attempt against an invoice, with the organisation's gateway
- `payment_gateway_invoice_verify_payment`: Verify a gateway payment against an invoice and record what it collected
