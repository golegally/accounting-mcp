# Products

- `products_list`: Retrieves the product list
- `products_create`: Create a new product
- `products_bulk_delete_products`: Delete multiple products
- `products_validate_bulk_delete_products`: Validate which products can be deleted
- `products_get`: Get product details
- `products_edit`: Edit the given product
- `products_delete`: Delete the given product
- `products_activate`: Activate the given product
- `products_get_product_bills`: Retrieve product associated bill transactions
- `products_get_product_estimates`: Retrieve product associated estimate transactions
- `products_inactivate`: Inactivate the given product
- `products_get_product_invoices`: Retrieve product associated invoice transactions
- `products_list_prices`: List all prices for a product
- `products_create_price`: Create a new price on a product
- `products_get_product_receipts`: Retrieve product associated receipt transactions
- `products_get_upload_url`: Get a presigned S3 PUT URL for uploading a product image
