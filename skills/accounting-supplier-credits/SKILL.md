# Supplier Credits

- `vendor_credits_get_vendor_credits`: List supplier credits
- `vendor_credits_create_vendor_credit`: Create a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_bulk_delete_vendor_credits`: Delete multiple supplier credits
- `vendor_credits_validate_bulk_delete_vendor_credits`: Validate which supplier credits can be deleted
- `vendor_credits_get_vendor_credit`: Get a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_edit_vendor_credit`: Edit a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_delete_vendor_credit`: Delete a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_get_applied_bills`: List the bills a supplier credit is applied to
- `vendor_credits_delete_apply_vendor_credit_to_bill`: Take a supplier credit back off a bill
- `vendor_credits_apply_vendor_credit_to_bills`: Apply a supplier credit to the supplier's open bills
- `vendor_credits_open_vendor_credit`: Issue a draft supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_create_refund`: Record a refund received against a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_delete_refund`: Delete a refund of a supplier credit
- `vendor_credits_get_refunds`: List a supplier credit's refunds
- `vendor_credits_void_vendor_credit`: Void an issued supplier credit
