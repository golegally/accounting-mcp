# Supplier Documents

- `supplier_documents_of_bill`: A bill beside its supplier's document at the government
- `supplier_documents_answer`: Accept, reject or keep pending a bill's supplier's document
- `supplier_documents_check`: Check the answer sent for a bill's supplier's document
- `supplier_documents_read`: Read a bill's registration's suppliers' documents from the government
- `supplier_documents_customer_response`: An invoice's customer's answer at the government, as kept
- `supplier_documents_read_customer_response`: Read an invoice's customer's answer at the government
- `supplier_documents_rules`: Whether a registration answers its suppliers' documents
