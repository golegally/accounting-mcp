# TaxResolution

- `tax_resolution_list_tax_codes`: The tax codes a product can be classified under
- `tax_resolution_sync_tax_codes`: Add the tax codes this organisation is missing
- `tax_resolution_resolve`: Resolve the tax treatment for one sale or purchase
- `tax_resolution_resolve_batch`: Resolve the tax treatment for many lines in one call
