# Vendor Advances

- `vendor_advance_transactions_list`: List movements of vendor advances
- `vendor_advances_balances`: Get a vendor's advances
- `vendor_advances_record`: Record an advance paid to a vendor
- `vendor_advances_apply`: Apply a vendor's advance to their bills
- `vendor_advances_unapply`: Take an advance application back off its bill
- `vendor_advances_delete_payment`: Delete a payment into a vendor's advance
- `vendor_advances_refund`: Record a vendor's advance paid back
